### 5 Important Principles

**1. Start with simple solutions**

We should not make the AI system complicated unless it is really needed. A simple workflow is easier to understand, test, and maintain.

**Team Rule:**
Start with the simplest AI workflow that can solve the problem. Add complexity only when required.

---

**2. Give the AI a clear task**

An AI agent works better when the task and expected result are clearly defined. Vague instructions can lead to poor or unexpected results.

**Team Rule:**
Every AI task should have a clear goal, instructions, and expected output.

---

**3. Give the AI the right tools**

Agents can perform better when they have access to the tools and information needed for their task. We should give an agent only the tools that are useful for its work.

**Team Rule:**
Give each AI agent only the tools and information it needs to complete its assigned task.

---

**4. Keep humans involved**

AI-generated work should not automatically be accepted. Team members should review the output and make the final decision.

**Team Rule:**
All important AI-generated work must be reviewed by a human before it is accepted.

---

**5. Test and improve the system**

AI agents may not always give the correct result. We need to test their output and improve the workflow when problems are found.

**Team Rule:**
Test AI outputs regularly, record important mistakes, and improve the workflow based on the results.

### Final Team Rules

Based on the resource, our team will follow these five rules:

1. **Keep AI workflows simple.**
2. **Give AI clear tasks and expected outputs.**
3. **Provide the right tools and context.**
4. **Keep humans responsible for final decisions.**
5. **Test AI work and improve continuously.**
